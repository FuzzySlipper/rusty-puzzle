# Asset contract: marker or glyph

A board marker (selection ring, legal-target mark, move preview, exit sign) or
a small interface glyph, seen straight down.

- A single centred symbolic shape on a fully transparent background (real
  alpha, never a painted checkerboard pattern), made of one or two flat
  colours with a thin warm outline, so it reads over both checker shades and
  under a piece.
- Must stay distinct from tile ornament and from pieces: geometric and
  bright where tiles are organic and muted.
- No letters, numerals or pseudo-writing.
- Square canvas, about 512 × 512.
