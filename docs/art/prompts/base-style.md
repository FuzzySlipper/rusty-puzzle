# Base style

Art nouveau fantasy for a patient, turn-based party puzzle on a square grid.
The world is warm and lush: a high fantasy of gardens, carved stone, gilded
ironwork, stained glass and travelling heroes, seen as if designed by a
fin-de-siècle decorative artist. It is adventurous and generous, not cozy
cuteness and never grim: no skulls, gore, rot, ruin-as-decay, sickly palettes
or oppressive darkness.

- **Line and form:** whiplash curves, flowing organic contours, botanical
  motifs (lilies, irises, vines, leaves, peacock eyes) and elegant
  asymmetry held inside a calm, readable overall shape. Ornament frames and
  decorates; it never hides the subject's silhouette.
- **Palette:** warm and saturated but harmonious. Deep teal and peacock
  green, warm ochre and honey gold, terracotta and madder rose, ivory and
  cream, plum and lapis accents. Darks are warm (umber, deep green), never
  neutral black. Gold is an accent, not a coating.
- **Figures:** heroic but approachable, slightly elongated proportions,
  readable faces, clothing with flowing hems and a few large decorative
  shapes. Each character carries one bold identifying prop.
- **Surfaces:** flat or gently modelled colour regions, no photographic
  texture, grain, grime, scratches or scan damage.
- **Never:** text, letters, numerals, signatures, watermarks, logos or
  pseudo-writing; borrowed characters or designs from existing games or
  illustrators; photorealism; 3D render look.
